## HP product

under $60,000, ships **early 2027**. Segment metadata reads Commercial.SMB.

**MJF 1200 Early Access** — no upfront payment, no commitment, 20% off MJF parts capped
at $2,000, up to $10,000 credit earned as 20% of parts spend, redeemable only at GA.
Parts via HP-branded Craftcloud. Vanity URL hp.com/getMJF1200.

> **The mechanic matters more than the machine.** It parks prospects in HP's funnel for

500C printhead, 195C heated chamber, PEEK/PAEK/Ultem 9085. **Open materials, explicitly
anti-lock-in.** Direct overlap with Stratasys FDM. List price not published.

**Metal Jet** — sintering education play. OptiPowder Ni718 qualification with Continuum
Powders and INDO-MIM.

## HP's standing operational weakness

HP habitually runs ads with expired dates still in the copy. As of Aug 18 2026: an
April 14 teaser and a February AAOP booth ad were both still live, each flagged for a
second consecutive run.

This is a usable talking point about operational rigor, and it is evidence against the
"HP out-markets us" framing. Check it every run and report how long each stale creative
has been running.
