# Standing facts

Carry these forward. **Verify, do not re-derive.** Re-researching settled facts every
week is the largest avoidable cost in the run.

Anything here that a run refutes should be moved to the bottom section, not deleted.

## HP product

**MJF 1200** — announced Apr 14 2026 at RAPID+TCT (Boston, Apr 13-16). 12 L build,
under $60,000, ships **early 2027**. Segment metadata reads Commercial.SMB.

**MJF 1200 Early Access** — no upfront payment, no commitment, 20% off MJF parts capped
at $2,000, up to $10,000 credit earned as 20% of parts spend, redeemable only at GA.
Parts via HP-branded Craftcloud. Vanity URL hp.com/getMJF1200.

> **The mechanic matters more than the machine.** It parks prospects in HP's funnel for
> nine months at zero cost to them. Any prospect who signs up is effectively pre-committed
> before we get a second meeting. This belongs in the sales brief whenever it appears.

**IF 600 HT** — HP's first FFF platform, GA US/Canada Apr 14 2026. 380x380x420mm,
500C printhead, 195C heated chamber, PEEK/PAEK/Ultem 9085. **Open materials, explicitly
anti-lock-in.** Direct overlap with Stratasys FDM. List price not published.

**Metal Jet** — sintering education play. OptiPowder Ni718 qualification with Continuum
Powders and INDO-MIM.

## Formlabs — carry forward (est. Aug 25 2026)

**Fuse X1.** Announced Jun 9 2026, ships **Q4 2026**, from **$84,999**.
330 x 330 x 565 mm. Nylon 12 + Open Material Mode at launch; Nylon 11 end of 2026;
Nylon 12 GF and TPU announced. Adaptive Thermal Control, AI-Powered Print Intelligence.
~1.3 sq m footprint, fits a standard industrial door, ~1 hour install.
Claims up to 50% lower cost per part and 3x throughput vs comparable industrial PBF
`[Unverified]` — no named comparator or test method.

**The timing fact that matters:** Fuse X1 ships **Q4 2026**, HP MJF 1200 ships
**early 2027**. Formlabs reaches the accessible-industrial segment first.

**Corporate.** Weighing a ~$500M IPO (Bloomberg, Aug 7 2026), SoftBank-backed.
Dan Riccio, former Apple hardware chief, took a stake and became strategic adviser
Aug 12 2026 amid a board reshuffle. Says three more printer platforms ship within
12 months after a record Q3. HQ Somerville MA. CRO is **Nick Graham**, who personally
fronts their thought-leader ads.

**Confirmed drone footprint.** Published case study: **ORQA** (Croatia) runs Fuse 1 and
Fuse 1+ 30W on Nylon 12 and TPU 90A for end-use drone parts. ORQA US LLC is in
Gauntlet II. This is a confirmed competitor placement inside a Gauntlet account.

**Unresolved and high-value.** Formlabs outbound claims **SkyFall**, Ukraine's largest
drone manufacturer, uses its printers. SkyFall independently builds the **Shrike 10F**
that won Gauntlet I for **Skycutter** (99.3/100). Neither source confirms the other.
`[Unverified]`. Verifying this is the highest-value open question on the board.

## HP's standing operational weakness

HP habitually runs ads with expired dates still in the copy. As of Aug 18 2026: an
April 14 teaser and a February AAOP booth ad were both still live, each flagged for a
second consecutive run.

This is a usable talking point about operational rigor, and it is evidence against the
"HP out-markets us" framing. Check it every run and report how long each stale creative
has been running.
