Strategic readout for Stratasys

What to do differently given this competitor behaves nothing like HP.

  1. There is no paid demand-generation contest with 3D Systems right now. Their entire observable footprint is 27 Google ads from a Korean subsidiary and a Japanese reseller, plus one automated jobs post. Any share-of-voice budget aimed at them is wasted.
  2. The contest is on programmes and specification, not media. The $9M Air Force follow-on, the Saudi NAMI military manufacturing licence and the Littleton expansion are all won through contracts, joint ventures and qualification. That is where they are taking defence ground, and it is invisible in advertising.
  3. Their real hardware momentum is metal. DMP 350 up about 90% and more metal printers sold in H1 2026 than in all of 2025. If we are defending polymer, we are defending the wrong flank against this competitor.
  4. Healthcare is their strongest segment and their most defensible. Largest segment, up 6.8%, Med Tech above 20%, NextDent with full-scope EU MDR. Yet it gets almost no paid support, which means clinical and channel relationships are carrying it.
  5. The CEO transition is a timing window. Graves is leaving with no named successor and a search running into later this year. [Inference] Large go-to-market changes are unlikely to start mid-search, so the next two quarters are more predictable than usual.
