Strategic readout for Stratasys

What to do differently given this competitor behaves nothing like HP. Updated for this run.

  1. Still no paid demand-generation contest with 3D Systems. Their footprint contracted again this week (27 to 26 Google ads), still just two APAC accounts plus one automated jobs post. Any share-of-voice budget aimed at them stays wasted.
  2. The contest remains on programmes and specification. No new contract or JV news was confirmed this week, but the $9M USAF follow-on and the Saudi NAMI licence from the prior two weeks still stand as the operative examples of how they actually win defence ground.
  3. Their real hardware momentum is still metal, per Q2: DMP 350 up about 90%, more metal printers sold in H1 2026 than all of 2025. If we are defending polymer against this competitor, we are defending the wrong flank.
  4. The CEO transition window is still open with no successor named. [Inference] Large go-to-market changes remain unlikely to start mid-search, so near-term predictability holds for a second run.
  5. New this week: analyst views are diverging further (Craig-Hallum Buy $6 vs. a reported Cantor Fitzgerald cut to $3.75). [Unverified timing] Worth a direct check next run since it signals disagreement on whether the turnaround story is credible.
