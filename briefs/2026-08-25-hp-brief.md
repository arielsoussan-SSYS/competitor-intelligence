# Competitive sales brief - HP Additive - Aug 25 2026

Run 2026-08-25, baseline 2026-08-18. Read this before any MJF 1200 or drone conversation.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**HP put 500,000 to 1,000,000 impressions behind a single MJF 1200 teaser video in nineteen days, and they are now running a dated Open House on October 1 to close it.**

That teaser ran March 26 to April 13 and delivered 97% into the United States. At a $30-80 B2B CPM that is roughly **$15,000 to $80,000 for one flight** `[Estimate]`.

The important read is not the number, it is the shape. **HP does not out-market us with steady presence. They concentrate budget into launch bursts and then go quiet.** Between bursts their volume is unremarkable: 18 creatives on one platform.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"We've reserved an HP MJF 1200. No payment needed, plus 20% off parts and up to $10,000 in credit."**

Take the offer seriously, because it is a good one. These are the real terms, now sourced from the ad itself: priority access before general availability, no upfront payment, 20% off eligible MJF parts orders through HP's own 3D Printing Service, credit toward a future machine, up to $10,000.

Then ask the question the offer avoids. **The machine does not ship until early 2027.** What are they producing between now and then? That 20% parts discount routes their production volume into HP's service bureau, so for the next two quarters they are outsourcing parts and building a dependency, not building a capability. And the credit only redeems against a machine that does not exist yet — ask what happens to it if the date slips.

**"HP is everywhere. Their marketing is far ahead of yours."**

Be precise instead of defensive. HP runs 18 creatives on one platform, 36 live instances, and 97% of their biggest flight landed in one country. What they do genuinely well is **concentrate** around a launch. That is a budget decision, and it is buyable.

Meanwhile three of their currently live ads carry expired dates: an AAOP booth ad for an event that ran in February, the "Join us on April 14" teaser, and an Orthotics and Prosthetics webinar still advertising **October 30, 2025**. That is ten months stale. Volume is not rigor.

**"HP is committed to drones."**

Fair, and unchanged. 14 of 36 instances, 39% of their volume, exactly as last week. No new drone creative in seven days. They are holding position, not escalating.

---

## 3. Where HP is vulnerable

- **The gap between the offer and the shipment.** Everything they are selling right now is a reservation against an early-2027 machine. That window is our best ground and it closes when they ship.
- **Stale creative, third consecutive run.** Three live ads with expired dates, one from October 2025.
- **They went quiet on prosthetics.** The emotive O&P carousel dropped this week; that theme fell from 5 instances to 2.
- **Concentration cuts both ways.** A burst strategy means long quiet stretches. Their April flight is five months old and still in rotation with a dead date on it.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **The MJF 1200 motion is now a full funnel.** Teaser, launch, reservation offer with real money attached, and now a room to walk into on October 1. That is a complete, competent campaign and we should say so internally rather than pretend otherwise.
- **The Open House is geographically targeted at Pacific Northwest manufacturing leaders.** That is more precise targeting than HP AM has shown before.
- **"Building a production system the business can trust"** is still running. That is our language.
- **Reshoring and Made in America.** Their UAV webinar and drone thought-leadership own the domestic-production frame in additive. With NDAA FY2026 in play, that frame is about to get more valuable, not less.

---

## 5. Accounts and actions

**October 1, HP MJF 1200 Open House, Pacific Northwest.** If we have coverage in that territory, this is an event to plan against, not just observe. Find out which of our accounts are attending.

**Anyone who mentions an MJF 1200 reservation** is in a two-quarter gap where they have no machine and are buying parts from HP. That is a live displacement window with a known expiry.
