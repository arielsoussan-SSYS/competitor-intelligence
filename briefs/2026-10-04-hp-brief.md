# Competitive sales brief - HP Additive - Oct 4 2026

Run 2026-10-04, baseline 2026-09-22. Read this before any MJF 1200 or drone conversation.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**HP cut its two most direct drone ads this week, including the one that let drone teams book time with an HP Application Engineer, and has launched nothing new in four weeks.**

Drone weight falls from 14 to 12 of HP's 34 live LinkedIn ads. What is left on drones is two webinars recorded in October 2025 and a reshoring video. `[Inference]` HP is coasting on drones exactly while the Gauntlet II winners are placing production orders.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"We've reserved an HP MJF 1200."**

Unchanged terms: no upfront payment, 20% off MJF parts (capped at $2,000), up to $10,000 credit. The machine still ships early 2027. Ask what they are producing until then, and point out that the parts discount routes their volume into HP's own service bureau for two quarters.

**"HP is the drone additive leader."**

HP just pulled its engineer-led drone offer and its Unusual Machines FPV case study. Offer what they withdrew: direct engineering time on the customer's parts, now.

**"HP's marketing is everywhere."**

16 creatives, one platform, effectively US only. Four live ads carry expired dates, including the Oct 1 Open House, which is over and still running. Volume is not rigor.

---

## 3. Where HP is vulnerable

- **No drone bottom-funnel offer.** The only drone call to action left is webinar registration.
- **Stale creative, now four ads:** AAOP (Feb 2026), O&P webinar (Oct 2025), #MJFNext (Apr 14 2026), Open House (Oct 1 2026).
- **The reservation gap.** Everything sold today is a promise against an early-2027 ship date.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **Production scale.** ADDMAN announced on Sep 30 that it will add 81 HP Jet Fusion 5620 Pro printers, taking its fleet past 125, with aerospace a target sector. Expect HP to cite it in A&D deals.
- **"Building a production system the business can trust"** is still in rotation. That is our language.
- **Reshoring.** HP's "Made in America" UAV webinar still owns the domestic-production frame in their ads.
