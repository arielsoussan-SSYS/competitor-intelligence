# Competitive sales brief - HP Additive - Oct 30 2026

Run 2026-10-30, baseline 2026-10-12. Read this before any MJF 1200 or drone conversation.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**HP has pulled every drone ad from LinkedIn.** Four creatives and 13 instances left since Oct 12, taking HP from 34 instances to 24. None of the 24 that remain is about drones.

`[Inference]` The drone webinars were months old and were retired. HP has not replaced them with a drone offer, and it dropped its engineer-routing drone offer in September.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"We've reserved an HP MJF 1200."**

HP still takes reservations, with a credit and parts discount earned through its own printing service. General availability is still early 2027. Ask what they produce until then, and what happens to the credit if the date slips.

**"HP is the drone additive leader."**

HP has no drone ad on LinkedIn today. The webinars that named Blueflite, Unusual Machines and Firestorm Labs are gone. Ask for production volumes, and offer what HP no longer does: direct engineering time on the customer's parts.

**"HP's marketing is everywhere."**

13 creatives, one platform, effectively US only. Three live ads carry expired dates, including an Open House that ended Oct 1.

---

## 3. Where HP is vulnerable

- **No drone presence on LinkedIn at all**, while Gauntlet II winners place production orders.
- **Stale creative, three ads:** AAOP (Feb 2026), #MJFNext (Apr 2026), Open House (Oct 1 2026, now 29 days over).
- **The reservation gap.** Everything sold is a promise against an early-2027 ship date.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **In-house production pitch.** HP's new Nov 5 webinar sells the MJF 1200 as industrial quality for "more workspaces and budgets" to buyers weighing outsourcing. `[Inference]` That is the buyer an F-series sale reaches.
- **"Building a production system the business can trust"** is still in rotation. That is our language.
- **Industrial filament.** IF 600 HT overlaps Stratasys FDM directly; three creatives still run.
