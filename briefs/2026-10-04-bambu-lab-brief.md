Run 2026-10-04, baseline 2026-08-25.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**On Sep 18 2026 a Texas jury found four Stratasys patents valid and infringed by Bambu Lab, with about $27.6M in past damages.**

This is our own litigation. **State the facts only, never characterise the case or predict what happens next, and route every customer question to Legal.** Bambu has said it will appeal. A second case has no trial date. Later PTAB rulings invalidated claims that Law360 says relate to $2.8M of the award.

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"Bambu does everything you do at a tenth of the price."**

Do not argue price. Ask where the machine will be used. Under NDAA FY2026 sections 849 and 880, Chinese-made, Chinese-software or China-networked additive machines are being shut out of defense work. Section 849 takes effect around Dec 18 2026 `[Inference, confirm the date with Legal]`.

**"What's going on with your lawsuit against Bambu?"**

"There was a jury verdict in September. I'm not the right person to comment on it; I can put you in touch with our team." Then move on.

- **Defense.** Every Bambu printer on a defense or Tier-1 floor has about eleven weeks before s849 applies `[Inference]`.
