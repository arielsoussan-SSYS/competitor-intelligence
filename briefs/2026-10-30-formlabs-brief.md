# Competitive sales brief - Formlabs - Oct 30 2026

Run 2026-10-30, baseline 2026-10-12. Read this before any drone, SLS or Fuse X1 conversation.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**Formlabs cut its LinkedIn rotation roughly in half.** Instances fell from 27 to 13 and creatives from 12 to 9 since Oct 12, the fourth straight fall (38, 33, 28, 27, 13).

`[Inference]` This is a deliberate prune of its April creatives, not a pause. Drones are now 3 of 13 instances (23%, up from 15%), so drones are a bigger share of a smaller program.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"Skyfall prints flight-ready drone parts the same day on our machines."**

Still `[Unverified]`, no published Skyfall case study. The ad is still listed 53 days after its recorded end date (Sep 7), so do not brief it as closed or as active.

**"The Fuse X1 prints 20 quadcopter frames in one build."**

The Fuse X1 was announced June 9 and is expected to ship in Q4 2026. No firm ship date found, and resellers list it as "ships when available". Ask when theirs arrives and what they use until then.

---

## 3. Where Formlabs is vulnerable

- **A shrinking LinkedIn program.** The quality, ease-of-use and "built to grow" ads are gone.
- **The Fuse X1 gap.** Ads promise capability for a machine without a firm ship date.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **Drone SLS messaging at steady weight** (3 of 13 instances), with a personalised drone message ad still listed.
- **EOS is also buying "SLS drone parts"** (3 creatives in a keyword scan). Not tracked yet.
