# Competitive sales brief - 3D Systems - Oct 4 2026

Run 2026-10-04, baseline 2026-09-22.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**3D Systems has started buying US search ads again.** A verified US corporate Google account appeared with 9 ads, the first in five runs: dental lab printers, dentures, metal printers, alloy materials, industrial SLA, and "Industrial 3D Printing Leader. Get a quote today." Last shown Oct 2.

Still nothing on LinkedIn except a recruitment post.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"3D Systems has cheaper dentures per part."** Their new ad says "Lower Cost Per Implant Part". Ask for the cost-per-part calculation and what is included in it.

**"3D Systems is the industrial 3D printing leader."** That line is from their new search ad. Ask who will support the machine through their leadership transition: the CEO is stepping down once a successor is named.

---

## 3. Where 3D Systems is vulnerable

- **Leadership in transition.** No successor named, and no press release since Aug 27.
- **Small and search-only.** 9 ads is a test, not a programme.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **Dental and metal search results.** Our buyers searching for dental lab or metal printers will now see 3D Systems.
- **Defense contracts.** Their real competition is contract awards (a $9M US Air Force metal system in August), which ad tracking cannot see.
