# Competitive sales brief - Formlabs - Oct 4 2026

Run 2026-10-04, baseline 2026-09-22.

---

## 1. The one thing to know

**Formlabs is moving budget from LinkedIn to Google search.** LinkedIn fell for the second run in a row (38, 33, now 28 ads) while its Google search ads rose to 121 from 106. `[Inference]` They are harvesting buyers who are already searching, ahead of Fuse X1 deliveries in Q4.

---

## 2. What you will hear, and what to say

**"Fuse X1 is shipping this quarter at $84,999."**

Formlabs still says "deliveries starting Q4 2026". We found no confirmation that any have shipped. Ask for a delivery date in writing and a reference site running it in production.

**"Formlabs works with drone teams, Skyfall uses their printers."**

That claim comes from Formlabs's own outbound message ad and is still `[Unverified]`. Do not repeat it. Ask the prospect which printers their supplier actually runs.

---

## 3. Where Formlabs is vulnerable

- **The Fuse X1 ship date is unproven.** Every Fuse X1 conversation is about a machine nobody has received yet.
- **Their message ad still runs past its own end date** (record says it ended Sep 7).
- **The case studies are gone from LinkedIn.** The three documents dropped this week were their proof points.

---

## 4. Where we are exposed

- **Drone SLS messaging is crowded.** On the "SLS drone parts" keyword, EOS now runs the most ads, then Phillips Machine Tools (a Formlabs reseller). Both drone ads Formlabs runs held steady.
- **Search.** A buyer typing production or SLS terms is now more likely to see Formlabs first.

---

## 5. Accounts and actions

**ORQA US** (`Locked` to Formlabs at the Croatian plant) just placed 2nd in Gauntlet II Close Quarters and is starting full production at a new Florida plant this month. `[Inference]` The US equipment decision may still be open. Worth a field call.
