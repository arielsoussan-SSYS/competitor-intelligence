Strategic signals for the Stratasys sales team. Focus: drones/UAV, new products, healthcare, events.

DRONES/UAV HP Drone Strategy

  • Unusual Machines partnership -- HP MJF produces TPU parts for SkyLite FPV drones. Manufacturing by Forecast 3D (San Diego). US production positioning.
  • XPONENTIAL 2026 (Detroit), Booth #22008 -- HP showcased MJF-produced UAVs/UUVs for "aerial, underwater, and autonomous uncrewed systems," emphasizing lightweight geometries and consolidated assemblies.
  • Avular x Materialise [new] -- Avular (drone maker) partnered with Materialise, a certified HP partner, to compress prototype turnaround to 2-3 weeks and reach market in ~4 months.
  • "Category 1 Fixed-Wing UAV Airframes" positioning [new] -- HP is publishing feature content specifically on MJF for fixed-wing airframe production, a more technical/defense-adjacent angle than prior messaging.
  • Webinar CTA active -- "Made in America: Redefining UAV Production with Additive Manufacturing," co-hosted with Commercial UAV News. Panelists: Unusual Machines, Blueflite, Firestorm.
  • The Eye Above Project -- Bush Ranger anti-poaching drone built with MJF. Emotional narrative for defense/government audience.
  • LinkedIn ad activity -- Multiple active drone ads, incl. 2 running variants of the "Made in America" UAV webinar promo and the recurring FPV/Unusual Machines case study.
  • Dedicated landing page -- reinvent.hp.com/us-en-3dprint-drones

NEW PRODUCTS 2026 Product Launches

  • MJF 1200 -- Compact 12-liter build volume, under $60,000. Entry point to MJF portfolio. Full availability early 2027. Reservation program active.
  • IF 600 HT -- Industrial Filament printer for high-temp polymers (aerospace, automotive). GA in US/Canada. HP's FDM play.
  • 5600 High Productivity Mode -- 20% output increase on existing 5600 series.
  • Dual Tone technology -- White and grey two-tone printing. 5600 series first, late 2026.
  • OptiPowder Ni718 -- New Metal Jet material for high-density/strength metal production.

HEALTHCARE O&P / Medical Vertical

  • Medicare reimbursement milestone -- 3D printing recognized by DME MACs/PDAC as fabrication method for prosthetics. Major adoption barrier removed.
  • Invent Medical -- 100,000+ parts across 1,000+ hospitals using MJF.
  • Applications -- Scoliosis braces, cranial orthoses, ankle-foot supports, prosthetic arms, prosthetic sockets.
  • New material coming -- Adjustable rigidity/flexibility/foam density in single device. Announced at AOPA National Assembly.
  • LinkedIn ads -- "3D Printing Solutions for Healthcare and O&P" (Engineer's Corner) + Hero Gauntlet prosthetics carousel.

  • XPONENTIAL 2026 (Detroit) -- Drone/UAV/robotics focus. Showcased MJF for uncrewed systems.
  • AOPA National Assembly -- Healthcare/O&P focus. New material announcement.
  • Formnext 2026 (Frankfurt, Nov 17-20) -- Upcoming. Expect more product announcements.
