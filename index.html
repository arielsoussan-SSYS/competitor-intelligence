PRODUCT

  • MJF 1200 announced Apr 14 2026 at RAPID + TCT (Boston, Apr 13-16). 12 L build, 320 x 165 x 230 mm, under 12 h print time, HP 3D HR PA 12 only at launch, material approx $100/kg. Priced below $60,000 US and EU. Ships early 2027. Segment metadata reads Commercial.SMB. source
  • IF 600 HT industrial filament printer, HP's first FFF platform. Unveiled Formnext Nov 2025, GA US and Canada Apr 14 2026. 380 x 380 x 420 mm, 500C printhead, 195C heated chamber, three interchangeable modules (280 / 360 / 500C), PEEK, PAEK, Ultem 9085, PC, PA-CF. Open materials, explicitly anti-lock-in. NFC-tagged spools. First install: Haelvoet. List price not published. source
  • Metal Jet new materials Apr 2026: copper, M247LC nickel superalloy, WC-Co. OptiPowder Ni718 qualified with Continuum Powders and INDO-MIM, sintered density above 98%. Volkmann vPort entry-level powder handling for Metal Jet S100. INDO-MIM committed to 5 more S100 units. source

THE MECHANIC TO WATCH - MJF 1200 EARLY ACCESS

  • No upfront payment, no purchase commitment. 20% off MJF parts today through the HP 3D Printing Service, capped at $2,000. Up to $10,000 purchase credit earned as 20% of parts spend, redeemable only against an MJF 1200 at GA. Parts fulfilled via HP-branded Craftcloud. Vanity URL hp.com/getMJF1200. source
  • [Inference] This converts a 9-month availability gap into a lead-capture and revenue loop. An account evaluating entry-level polymer between now and early 2027 can sit in HP's funnel, spending with HP, without buying anything.
